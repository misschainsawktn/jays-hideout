**Evrenthall** is my fantasy worldbuilding project that I also use as the setting for most of my ventures into DMing for my friends. A total revamp of the project has been in progress since April 25th, 2025.

I prefer to keep the Evrenthall Wiki a separate entity from this site, out of personal preference and ease of browsing for my players. If you're interested, you can check it out [here](https://misschainsawktn.github.io/evrenthall-wiki/)! I would really appreciate it, considering how much work and effort I pour into it :) It's still a work in progress, but what personal project wiki isn't?
