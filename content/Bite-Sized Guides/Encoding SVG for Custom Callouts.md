AKA, my struggle with figuring out how to get the Kanban callout working as intended and for the icon not to display as a solid color block...

1. Go to https://lucide.dev/ and pick your icon
2. Click "Copy SVG"
3. Go to https://svgencode.com/ (I tried with the one suggested by the Quartz documentations, but couldn't get the icon to work anyway)
4. In the **URL-Encoding** section, copy the **URL-Encoded CSS** one
5. Remove the `background-image:` part
6. Profit!! It now works :3
