I thought, "hey, wouldn't it be fun to have a nice way of tracking down your little achievements in life and the milestones you reached?", so I made this page. It's very silly, but I feel like my brain could benefit from some sort of reward system, plus I will have something cute to look back on someday!

Achievements are listed in chronological order, and each entry contains a "sticker" (just a little decorative image), a small description, the date and a link to a note with some more additional details, just to track what I've done so far and how it made me feel.
