On **June 8th, 2025**, I went to vote for the first time! It wasn't an election vote, sure, but I still genuinely think referendums are just as important, considering that they are the main form of direct democracy in Italy. And this was my first one! Not because I didn't *want* to vote, but because I was a minor for all the other elections and referendums...

I was *very* excited about it... Even the lady at the urns noticed KFJFJHDSAK  
The process was a lot simpler than I thought, to be honest. Five pieces of paper (color-coded too, so it was pretty much impossible to get the urn they were going in wrong), a pencil, and a YES/NO question. I have no idea why I thought it would be harder. Still, it was pretty thrilling to *actually* express my opinion through voting! And now there's a nice stamp on my electoral card... The first of many, I hope!

Now, to be completely honest about my hopes, I really don't think the quorum of votes will be reached (which means the whole referendum will go down the drain, which really sucks in my opinion because the proposals were pretty important), but at least I know that I did my part and my duty as a citizen, even in a political climate where a bunch of our own politicians are telling us to Don't Go Vote Actually because they hate workers and immigrants and to them democracy is more of a suggestion than a tenet of the Constitution, I guess. I can't burden myself with other people's avoided responsibilities, after all.

(Sorry for the lil angry rant about politics... I just really don't like the current situation.)
