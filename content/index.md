Hello everyone, welcome to my little hideout on the World Wide Web, now made public just for funsies. My name is **Jay** (although my IRL friends know me as Ely, it's still me!) and I'm just a cute little bunny creature on the Internet, figuring out how to get her space in a world sometimes much too big and confusing.   
In real life, I am a fifth-year high school student approaching her final exam and nearing her entrance into the world of university. It's scary and exciting at the same time!

I enjoy many things, most of which fall somewhere into the sphere of somewhat nerdy interests. Most of all, I like creating things (whether they're written works or pieces of art), studying many different subjects, a variety of pieces of media in pretty much any form, and many things more you can discover by snooping around the site. I suppose I like talking about myself and my interests too, so there's that.

>[!warning] A lil note on language(s)
>Given that English is **not** my first language (although I do have a [C2 Proficiency qualification](https://www.cambridgeenglish.org/exams-and-tests/proficiency/) in it), some pages on this website might not be fully in English (or in English at all) and may contain random usage of Italian, my native language. It all depends on the mood I'm in and in what language I read/studied/played what I'm talking about in.

If you wish to communicate with me directly, feel free to reach out, I promise I do not bite! If anything, please excuse me if I don't reply immediately or if I come off as a bit awkward—I am often pretty low on energy and neurodivergent, things that impact my social skills significantly (but I am trying to get better at them!)


# 🔖 Tag Guide

I'm trying to personalize my tagging system based on how I visualize my mindspace. These are the "broader" tags: each note should have at least one of these linked to it.

#cupboard: Organization. **Cupboards** are map-of-content pages, with many different links attached to them.

#greenhouse: Scholastic and academic knowledge. **Greenhouses** must frequently be revisited and taken care of, if one wants them to flourish to their full potential.

#flowerbed: Personal knowledge. Sometimes related to Greenhouses, **Flowerbeds** are less structured, but still taken care of (if not a bit less frequently), and are mostly there as a hobby.

#bonfire: Personal creative efforts. **Bonfires** are projects I can sit around and work on at my own pace without worrying much, because they're for myself before they are for anyone else. Flames may dim, but they're never truly gone forever.

#creek: Media interests. **Creeks** are where I can go when I want to indulge into observing and reflecting on things other people have done.

Other tags are more specific. If I listed them all here, this page would become unnecessarily long, so you can either discover them by looking around or through the [[tags|Tag Index]].
