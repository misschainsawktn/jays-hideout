Hello everyone, welcome to the little space I take up on the World Wide Web. My name is **Jay** (although my IRL friends know me as Ely, it's still me!) and I'm a little bunny on the Internet, figuring out how to get her space in a world sometimes much too big and confusing. In real life, I am a fifth-year high school student approaching her final exam and nearing her entrance into the world of university.

I enjoy many things, most of which fall somewhere into the sphere of somewhat nerdy interests. I like creating things (whether they're written works or pieces of art), studying many different subjects, a variety of pieces of media in pretty much any form, and many things more you can discover by snooping around the site. I suppose I like talking about myself and my interests too, so there's that.

>[!warning] A lil note on language(s)
>Given that English is **not** my first language (although I do have a [C2 Proficiency qualification](https://www.cambridgeenglish.org/exams-and-tests/proficiency/) in it), some pages on this website might not be fully in English (or in English at all) and may contain random usage of Italian, my native language. It all depends on the mood I'm in and in what language I read/studied/played what I'm talking about in.

If you wish to communicate with me directly, feel free to reach out, I promise I do not bite! If anything, please excuse me if I don't reply immediately or if I come off as a bit awkward—I am often pretty low on energy and neurodivergent, things that impact my social skills significantly (but I am trying to get better at them!)
