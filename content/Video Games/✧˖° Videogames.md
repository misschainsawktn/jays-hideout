I enjoy taking notes about games I play. Almost a bit too much. Most of it is because there is something so inherently interesting to me in the act of keeping track of all I discover as I play, some of it is because I am absolutely terrible at remembering things if I don't jot them down somewhere, and a bit is because I just feel the urge to catalog things in tiny little compartments so that I can revisit them once I need them... This is a philosophy that can be applied to most things to be fair, I just also love applying it to videogames!!

Games are listed in alphabetical order, just to make finding them easier.
