> [!example|wm-sm right] GLYPH REFERENCES
> - P = Power (AD Power; AD x; Dimboost mult. x; Buy 10 mult. x)
> - I = Infinity (ID Power; IP x; Infinities x; Infinity Power Conversion)
> - R = Replication (Replicanti mult. Power; DT x for 1e10,000 Replicanti; Replication Speed; Replicanti Power per Glyph Level)
> - T = Time (TD Power; EP x; Eternities x; Game Speed)
> - D = Dilation (AD Power while Dilated; DT x; Passive TT/Hour; TG Treshold)
> - E = Effarig (~~max 4/7 possible effects, 1 and 2 mutually exclusive~~ as of Ra, 7/7 effects)
> - R = Reality
> - C = Cursed
> ---
> **EFFARIG GLYPH EFFECTS:**
> - 1 = RM multiplier
> - 2 = Glyph Instability delay
> - 3 = Game Speed power
> - 4 = Achievement Bonus multiplier
> - 5 = Buy 10 power
> - 6 = All Dimensions multiplier
> - 7 = Antimatter Production exponent
