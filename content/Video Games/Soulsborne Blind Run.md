My boyfriend is a great Dark Souls (and Bloodborne) (and Elden Ring) connoisseur, I know absolutely nothing about any of those games but I like piecing together the lore and plot of things.  
And thus, the **Great Dark Souls (and Bloodborne) (and Elden Ring) Veteran x Blind Run** was born: my boyfriend plays the games and hints me towards interesting things, I watch him play, take notes and speculate about what's happening.  
In general, I love this specific "format" of experiencing a game because it allows me to piece lore elements together without stressing out over not being good enough at playing it (a lot of games really make my anxiety spike up when playing them, despite me otherwise really enjoying them), while also giving me someone else to directly discuss the lore implications and my theories with. It just heightens the entire experience!

These are digitized versions of the notebooks containing all my notes about the lore of the games. I apologize in advance for the enormous clusters of nodes that will result...
