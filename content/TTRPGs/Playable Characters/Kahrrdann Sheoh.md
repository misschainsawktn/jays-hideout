# Character Information

## Appearance

The first thing one would see upon meeting Kahrrdann would be her suit of armor. Broad, tall, made of iron shiny enough to glisten in the light yet clearly worn and burnt, with all its visible tubes and gears distinguishing it from most other armor.  
The first thing one would notice would be the heat and the light. Radiating both from just beneath the steel, 

Whenever Kahrrdann happens to doff her armor, the sight has a tendency to be somewhat... Underwhelming. One would expect an equally huge creature to come out of that mountain of steel, yet Kahrrdann's pure elemental component is relatively average, a bit on the lither side
## Personality



## History

Kahrrdann's tale begins in the moment of her creation at the hands of Sybrig, a powerful sorceress fueled by obsession towards her goal of creating a perfect lifeform, one that would obey her every command without question, that wouldn't dare ever leave her side, that would make her akin to a God. Of all her experiments made on Elementals and suits of armor, Kahrrdann was the first successful one
