All my study notes and summaries, now made digital. I take the original ones of standard notebooks because I've noticed I retain information a lot better when [[Writing by Hand|writing it by hand]], and then copy them over here so that they're always accessible even if my notebooks aren't with me (plus all the added benefits of being able to actually see links between things and organizing notes more cleanly and easily).

Each notebook folder is designated through the scholastic/academic year I have taken those notes in and a small designation signifying the school/university I go or went to. These start from my fifth year of high school for no other reason than because that's when I started building this hideout KFDGSHJHGD
